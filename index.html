Sites

Seed dataset, researched from public reporting (Brooklyn Paper, Greenpointers, Gothamist, Brownstoner, NYC Mayor's Office/DOT/HPD releases, and others) as of September 2026. Pin locations are geocoded against NYC Planning Labs' GeoSearch service (built on PAD/PLUTO). Broadway Triangle, Monitor Point, Fulton-Howard West, and the Park Ave/BQE corridor don't have single exact-address matches in that dataset. Those four pins sit at the nearest verified address on the same block or site, not a precise parcel centroid. This is not an official city dataset. History sections come from Wikipedia, NYC Parks, Landmarks Preservation Commission material, and local history sources. Some pre-1900s history could not be confirmed. Those gaps are noted in the popups. Bulk NYC Open Data (PLUTO) pulls are planned for a future version. See the note Griffin has on automation.
